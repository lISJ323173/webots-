Probe method
------------
A temporary copy (cart_pole_probe.wbt) added DEF CART, DEF POLE and a read-only
Supervisor controller. The controller sampled the two PositionSensors and the
cart/pole fields every 8 ms and wrote docs/a1-probe-run*.csv. The probe does not
apply forces; the original world remains passive with controller "<none>".
